### Data integrity and safe saves — batch 1 of 4 from the 2026-10-29 audit

The first of four batches that clear [AUDIT-2026-10-29.md](../AUDIT-2026-10-29.md). No schema change: everything here is a rule, a guard or a state the UI can now reach.

- **Archive instead of delete, for accounts and goals.** Both screens list archived rows in their own card with **Restore**; an archived account leaves every total, the Safe-to-Spend pool and the allocator but keeps its own balance and history, and an archived goal gets no slice of a paycheck, no funded progress and no earmark in the pool. Deleting a goal is a second, deliberate step from the archived list.
- **Account deletion counts its entries.** `RemoveAccountDialog` asks the main process how many entries the account holds and offers *Archive instead* / *Delete account and N entries* / Cancel. `deleteAccount` refuses a delete with entries unless `withEntries` is passed, and that path soft-deletes both legs of every transfer touching the account.
- **One sign rule for money.** `src/shared/kinds.ts` is the single table: income positive, expense/bill payment/transfer/goal funding/investment negative, adjustment either. The CSV parser derives the sign, the importer reports rows that still contradict their kind, and `createTransaction` normalises on write. `updateTransaction` refuses to turn an entry into a transfer or a transfer leg into something else, and mirrors date/amount/text edits onto the paired leg. The spreadsheet makes **Account** and **Kind** read-only on transfer rows.
- **Dialogs close only on success.** Every store mutation returns `Promise<boolean>`; the account, bill, goal, entry and payment dialogs stay open with their error when a save fails. Writes run through one serial queue, and `saveSettings` rolls back its optimistic value on failure.
- **Legacy adoption is atomic and verified** (`src/main/db/legacy.ts`): header check, staged copy, `PRAGMA quick_check` on a read-only handle, sidecars renamed first, cleanup on failure, and a dialog that says the old folder was left untouched.
- **CSV import fails loudly on an unclosed quote**, naming the line the quote starts on.
- **Exports read the whole ledger** (`listAllTransactions`) instead of the 500-row screen snapshot, and are written `0600`.
- **Tests:** four new files — `src/shared/kinds.test.ts`, `src/shared/csv.test.ts`, `src/main/db/legacy.test.ts`, `src/main/db/ledger-guards.test.ts` — 25 cases, `npm test` at **98 tests across twelve files**.

Verification: `npm test`, `npm run typecheck` and `npm run build` clean; the built app launched on a disposable `CFFIXED_USER_HOME` profile and driven over CDP — archive → archived card → restore for both an account and a goal, the remove dialog reading *"its 2 entries"* with all three choices, and the goal dialog staying open on a blank name then closing after a successful save.

### Accessibility, dialog behaviour and copy — batch 3 of 4 from the 2026-10-29 audit

The third batch. No schema change and no domain-math change: every fix is renderer behaviour, presentation or wording. It is recorded as [ADR 0014](decisions/0014-one-dialog-one-focus-model.md).

- **One dialog model.** `useDialog(onClose, active)` in `src/renderer/src/components/ui.tsx` now owns focus entry, the Tab/Shift-Tab trap, Escape, body scroll lock and focus return for the account, bill, goal, entry, erase, first-run and allocator dialogs. Focus returns to the element that opened the dialog — captured **during render**, before React's `autoFocus` moves it, which is the bug the first attempt shipped and then fixed. The allocator passes `closeIfIdle`, so Escape, the backdrop and the close button do nothing while a route is saving.
- **Backdrop dismissal is press-start plus click.** `backdropHandlers` remembers whether `mousedown` landed on the backdrop, so selecting text and overshooting the panel no longer discards the form.
- **Dialogs are named by their heading.** `Modal` and the allocator panel set `aria-labelledby` on the visible `<h2>` and `aria-describedby` on the subtitle via `useId`; the duplicated `aria-label` is gone.
- **Skip link and focus rings.** A **Skip to content** link precedes the sidebar and `<main>` is focusable; `:focus-visible` draws a 2px accent ring on every control, `.workspace`, `.modal` and `.allocator-panel` suppress the ring on their own containers, and `.alloc-income:focus-within` rings the whole income field.
- **Theme is a radio group and System is live.** Sidebar **System / Light / Dark** buttons sit in a `role="radiogroup"` with `aria-checked`, and the resolved state prints beside the label. `usePrefersLight` reads `matchMedia` through `useSyncExternalStore`, so an OS appearance change repaints without a restart. The footer renders plain `WageRoute` until `app:info` answers instead of flashing `v…`.
- **Toasts are live regions.** Success is `role="status"`, errors are `role="alert"` and no longer auto-expire; `pauseNotice`/`resumeNotice` stop the timer on hover or focus; a labelled **Dismiss notification** button replaces the whole-surface click, and `.toast-text` is selectable.
- **Contrast.** Light-mode `--text-muted`, `--placeholder`, `--text-faint`, `--accent` (now `#0a7a54`), `--savings`, `--investment`, `--goal`, `--debt` and `--leisure` were darkened to clear WCAG AA; dark-mode muted text was lifted for the same reason; `::selection` uses `var(--accent)`/`var(--accent-ink)`.
- **Layout and overflow.** `.table-scroll` lets the Priority Wall scroll inside its card at narrow widths; `.account-summary` drops to 2×2 under 1180px; account names in the ledger grid and the wall, and goal titles, ellipsize with a `title` tooltip; `.wall-table` sets a first-column width range.
- **Copy.** The spreadsheet subtitle is tab-aware (edit vs read-only forecast), the account-type hint reads *"Leave as Nothing in particular to keep it a general account"*, and a failed route says the paycheck was not routed and nothing was saved. Name inputs in the account, bill and goal dialogs carry `maxLength={60}`.
- **Import wizard steps.** The rail renders the current step as a non-interactive `span` with `aria-current="step"`, a `<button>` only for the fix step when it is reachable from review, and plain spans elsewhere — so no chip reopens the native picker and the current step is announced.
- **Per-row pending.** `BillPrompts` tracks `pendingKey` (`billId:dueOn`) instead of one global flag, so logging one payment disables only that row and shows *Logging…* only there.
- **The manual emergency target is not clobbered.** Saving any other setting used to write an empty draft over `emergencyManualTargetCents`; the goal dialog now writes the manual target only when manual mode is saved, and restores the stored value when switching from automatic to manual.

### Correctness, packaging hardening and typed errors — batch 4 of 4 from the 2026-10-29 audit

The fourth batch. Still schema 7, no new migration; it closes the forward-compatibility hole, the last three date/pointer validation gaps, the startup cost and three packaging weaknesses.

- **Impossible calendar dates are rejected** (`C-09`). `isISODate` round-trips through `Date.UTC` and compares year/month/day, so `2026-02-31`, `2026-13-01` and `0000-00-00` fail `assertDate` the same way the CSV importer already rejected them. `src/shared/dates.test.ts` covers leap days, month ends and the wrong shape.
- **A newer database is refused at launch** (`C-08`). `migrate()` throws `SchemaTooNewError` when `user_version > SCHEMA_VERSION`; the main process shows *"This database is from a newer WageRoute"* and quits without opening a window or writing to the file. `migrations.test.ts` asserts the error's code, the found/supported numbers and that `user_version` is unchanged.
- **A missing goal can no longer own an account** (`C-10`). `updateGoal` requires the goal before it opens the transaction, so `fundingAccountIds` cannot write `dedicated_goal_id` for a row that does not exist.
- **Typed repository errors** (`AR-4`). `src/shared/errors.ts` adds `RepositoryError` with a `VALIDATION` / `NOT_FOUND` / `CONFLICT` / `STATE` code and an optional field. Every throw in `repositories.ts` (25 sites) now carries one. Electron only carries `message` across IPC, so `encodeError` embeds the code in the message and `decodeError`, `apiErrorMessage` and `apiErrorCode` take it back out in the renderer; the store's toast text and the error boundary both route through `apiErrorMessage`.
- **The packaged build is hardened** (`SEC-2`, `SEC-3`). Electron fuses: `runAsNode` off, `NODE_OPTIONS` off, `--inspect` off, cookie encryption on, embedded ASAR integrity validation on, `onlyLoadAppFromAsar` on. The packaged window sets `devTools: false`, and the default Electron menu (reload, force-reload, toggle devtools) is replaced by a minimal macOS app/edit/window menu or removed on other platforms. `afterPack` became `afterSign` because the fuse flip after signing invalidated the ad-hoc signature — verified with `codesign --verify --deep --strict` on the rebuilt bundle.
- **Startup cost** (`PF-2`). `Spreadsheet`, `Allocator` and `ImportWizard` are `React.lazy` chunks behind one `Suspense`; the `react-data-grid` stylesheet moved from `main.tsx` into `TransactionsGrid.tsx`. Entry bundle 1,005 kB → 793 kB, grid chunk 170 kB, allocator chunk 38 kB.
- **Database tuning** (`PF-3`). `cache_size = -16000`, `temp_store = MEMORY`, `mmap_size = 268435456` on every connection, and the handle is closed if migration refuses the file.
- **Prompt scan** (`PF-4`). `dueBillPrompts` groups payments by `billId` once instead of scanning the payment list per bill.

Verification: `npm run typecheck` clean, `npm test` **127 passing across sixteen files**, `npm run build` clean. `npm run dist:mac` produced both DMGs; `npx @electron/fuses read` reported RunAsNode Disabled, EnableNodeOptionsEnvironmentVariable Disabled, EnableNodeCliInspectArguments Disabled, EnableCookieEncryption Enabled, EnableEmbeddedAsarIntegrityValidation Enabled, OnlyLoadAppFromAsar Enabled; `codesign --verify --deep --strict` passed. The packaged arm64 app launched on a fresh `CFFIXED_USER_HOME=/tmp/wageroute-b4` profile, loaded its renderer from `app.asar`, showed the first-run card and had no console errors. On the disposable dev profile (`/tmp/wageroute-b2`, schema 7): the Spreadsheet rendered 18 rows from its lazy chunk with the grid stylesheet applied (`display: grid`, header background from `react-data-grid`), the Add-entry dialog showed *"Enter an amount"* with no `[[wageroute:` marker anywhere in the document, and a rejected `window.api.updateGoal('missing-goal', …)` carried `[[wageroute:NOT_FOUND]] Goal not found` across the boundary. **Not verified:** the schema-too-new dialog itself (a native modal blocks the harness), Windows packaging, or the Intel DMG launching.

### Write safety, boot integrity and boundary hardening — batch 5

No schema change. This batch closes the data-safety and boundary findings the first four batches left open: what happens when the file is damaged or rewritten, what an export leaves behind when it fails, and who is allowed to call the ledger.

- **A copy before every migration** (`C-13`). `getDatabase` reads `user_version` after `quick_check` and, when it is below the build's schema, writes `userData/backups/pre-upgrade-<from>-to-<to>-<stamp>.db` with `VACUUM INTO` before `migrate` runs. `maintenance.ts` prunes to the newest three. A failed copy aborts the launch rather than migrating without one.
- **Integrity check on open** (`C-13`). `assertHealthy` runs `PRAGMA quick_check`; a non-SQLite file and a failed check both raise `DatabaseCorruptError`, which the main process shows as *"This ledger failed its integrity check"* with the file untouched, then quits.
- **Erase is physical** (`C-16`). `secure_delete = ON` on every connection, and `wipeAllData` ends with `VACUUM` + `wal_checkpoint(TRUNCATE)`. Deliberately **no** pre-erase copy: the two-step dialog tells the user everything is deleted, and keeping a silent copy would make that untrue. "Back up first" is what Export is for.
- **Atomic exports** (`C-34`). `src/main/files.ts` writes to `.<name>.<pid>.tmp` in the target's directory, `fsync`s, then renames. A failed export removes its temporary and leaves the previous file. The database file is `chmod 600` on open (`SEC-1`, best effort).
- **Import lifecycle** (`C-33`). The size guard compares `Buffer.byteLength`, not UTF-16 units, and `data:reset` clears the held-import map so a wizard cannot confirm into the new profile.
- **Unknown currency** (`C-36`). `isCurrencyCode` checks the platform's canonical list (`Intl.supportedValuesOf('currency')`, shape-only fallback); `parseSettings` falls back to USD, `writeSettings` refuses the value, and the backup importer reports the row as an issue.
- **Archived accounts** (`C-38`). `requireAccount` rejects an archived row for new money movement with *"That account is archived — restore it before using it here"*. The goal-funding path passes `allowArchived` so its own, more specific message survives.
- **One transaction per snapshot** (`C-37`), **allocation reads scoped by `user_id`** (`C-27`), **a round-up memo inside 240 characters and no decorative counter-account on non-transfers** (`C-39`), **CSV text columns defused against formula injection** (`C-32`), and **the demo seed's duplicate savings leg removed** (`C-28`).
- **IPC sender validation and a navigation allowlist** (`C-17`). `handle()` rejects a call whose `senderFrame` is not the packaged `index.html` (or the dev origin); `will-navigate` compares the exact file URL instead of the `file://` prefix.
- **A stricter packaged CSP and a crash log** (`P-82`, `C-48`). A Vite `transformIndexHtml` hook swaps the development policy for one without `'unsafe-inline'` scripts, localhost sockets or unrestricted object/base/form/frame sources. `src/main/log.ts` writes uncaught errors, unhandled rejections, process exits and boot failures to `userData/logs/main.log` (512 kB rotation, `0600`), and a boot failure now shows a dialog instead of leaving a windowless running app.
- **Autopay honesty** (`PV-5`, copy half only). The bill dialog says WageRoute never posts a payment. *Posting* scheduled bills is not implemented; see the open product work below.

Verification: `npm run typecheck` clean, `npm test` **138 passing across eighteen files**, `npm run build` clean, and `out/renderer/index.html` carries the production policy (`script-src 'self'`, no localhost). The built app ran on a fresh `CFFIXED_USER_HOME=/tmp/wageroute-b5`: `window.api.getSnapshot()` returned the seeded profile (5 accounts, 14 entries, savings at $8,700.00 with exactly two transfer legs) through the sender check, `window.api.resetData()` then returned 0 accounts, and `grep` found *"Blue Bottle"* in neither `wageroute.db` nor `wageroute.db-wal` afterwards. The Priority Wall's **Edit bill** dialog rendered the new autopay note. The first run of this batch also proved the crash log: the seed's decorative counter-account was refused, the rejection landed in `main.log`, and it exposed the windowless-boot path that the new dialog now covers. **Not verified:** the corrupt-database dialog itself (a native modal blocks the harness), and that a *rejected* IPC call is refused rather than accepted — the guard is exercised only in its allow direction.

### Core correctness and honesty sweep — batch 6

No schema change and no new screen. This batch is the C-series minor and nit findings: every place the core could quietly do the wrong thing with a number, a date, a name or a missing row.

- **The capped ledger grid admits what it hides.** The spreadsheet tab reads **Transactions (newest 500 of 520)**, a `.sheet-note` under the grid says the older entries are still counted in every balance, a CSV export and a backup, and the dashboard subtitle prints `transactionTotal` instead of the capped list length. `Snapshot.transactionTotal` is the new count (`transactionCount` in `repositories.ts`).
- **Safe to Spend counts settled cash.** `AccountWithBalance.cashCents` sums entries dated today or earlier and `safeToSpendPoolCents` uses it, so a deposit recorded for next Friday no longer inflates today's spendable pool; the account still shows its full `balanceCents`.
- **"Tight" means fully spoken for** (`C-43`). The leisure-under-10% heuristic is gone: a plan is tight when the bills consume the whole cheque, covered when anything is left. Saving 100% on purpose no longer reads as a warning.
- **The emergency average uses completed months.** `averageMonthlyVariableCents` windows back from the last day of the previous month and divides by the months actually covered, instead of counting the month in progress; the current month is the fallback until one month completes.
- **Money parsing refuses symbols and contradictions.** A bare `$`, `usd`, `-`, `.` or `(-5)` no longer posts `$0.00`; `USD 5`, `.5`, `+12.30` and `1,234` still parse. `formatMoneyCompact` actually uses compact notation with one fraction digit.
- **CSV line numbers are physical.** `parseCsvRows` returns `{ line, cells }`, so a quoted newline in an earlier row no longer shifts every later issue by one, and day-first slashed dates (`31/12/2026`) are read correctly while ambiguous ones stay month-first. An export falls back to the entry's joined `accountInstitution` so a filtered account list cannot blank the bank column.
- **Imported text is cleaned and a bad row no longer kills the file.** Memos are cut to 240 characters, categories to 60, control characters stripped, each trim reported against its line, and `insert.run` is wrapped per row so one constraint failure becomes an issue instead of an aborted import.
- **Missing rows and bad scalars are refused.** `updateRow`, `softDelete` and `deleteTransaction` raise `NOT_FOUND` instead of silently doing nothing; `deleteAccount` checks existence first; `assertDueDay` (1–31), `assertPaycheckIndex` (1st–4th), `assertSortOrder` and a strict `flagColumn` reject non-numbers and `"false"`, which used to be truthy; a transfer's edited amount can no longer be zero.
- **Smaller corrections.** `parseSettings` reads `1`/`0` as booleans so a restored backup keeps its flags; `buildAccountMap` returns a null-prototype map consulted with `Object.hasOwn`, so an account named `constructor` maps normally; `deleteAccount` only rewrites live bills, goals and paychecks; `settings:save` round-trips through `parseSettings` (the currency is still refused, not swapped); the preload contract now types the create calls as returning their new id and `confirmImport` as always returning a result; four dead exports (`advanceByFrequency`, `sumCents`, `safeCents`, `leisurePercent`) are deleted.
- **Tests.** Seventeen cases added across five existing files, `npm test` at **155 tests across eighteen files**.

Verification: `npm run typecheck`, `npm test` (155) and `npm run build` clean. The built app ran on a disposable `CFFIXED_USER_HOME` profile seeded with 520 entries, driven over CDP: the ledger tab rendered **Transactions (newest 500 of 520)**, the note under it named the 20 hidden entries, and the dashboard subtitle read **520 entries in the local ledger**. **Not verified:** none of the parsing or validation paths were exercised through the UI — they are covered by unit and repository tests only.

### Renderer, packaging and documentation sweep — batch 7

No schema change and no new domain rule. The remaining minor findings are presentation, keyboard semantics, copy and build plumbing, plus a documentation reconciliation pass against the schema-7 code.

- **Snoozing is per occurrence.** **Not yet** keys the prompt by `billId:dueOn`, the hidden rows move into a **Snoozed** list with **Bring back**, and next month's occurrence still asks.
- **The projections grid sorts.** Date, type, name, category and amount all sort with `aria-sort`, so a 90-day forecast can be read oldest-first or largest-first instead of only in due-date order.
- **The allocator and Settings only offer accounts a paycheck can land in.** The deposit lists are limited to checking and savings; with none the allocator shows **No account yet** and *"Add a checking or savings account, then reopen the allocator"* instead of an empty select.
- **Round-up reports its effective state.** The switch reads off when the saved destination is archived or is not a savings account, the select says that destination no longer exists, and a profile that never chose one is asked to choose plainly. The rule is spelled out as *a negative expense on a checking or cash account*.
- **The spreadsheet offers only kinds a manual entry can mean.** `bill_payment` and `goal_funding` are posted by the prompt and the goal card, so the Add entry dialog lists income, expense, transfer, investment and adjustment only; with no live account the grid shows **Add an account first** and **Add entry** is disabled.
- **The ledger grid takes the height it is given.** The Spreadsheet is a flex column instead of a grid sized by a guessed viewport offset, and the layout breakpoint moved from 980px to 1100px, above the 1060px minimum window.
- **Tints and selection colours follow the theme.** Hard-coded `rgba()` washes became `color-mix(in srgb, var(--token) …)`, and react-data-grid's selection uses the accent pair, so light mode no longer inherits dark washes.
- **Segmented controls are radio groups.** Theme, round-up increment and emergency mode expose `role="radiogroup"`/`radio` with `aria-checked`, roving `tabIndex` and arrow/Home/End keys; every progress bar carries a label; the donut's accessible name lists each slice's share.
- **Motion respects the system setting**, and animations collapse under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`.
- **The import review promises only what will be written.** Left-out rows are subtracted from the file's table counts, a write in progress announces itself, and a failed import prints its reason in the wizard along with the fact that nothing was written.

### Multi-platform installers: MSI, portable/zip and Linux — tasks #38 and #87

`origin/feat/linux-windows-installers` (`2ef251f`, held out of 1.1.0) was merged forward rather than rebased, keeping the hardened builder config from batches 1–6: fuses, `afterSign`, pinned DMG and NSIS names and the NSIS options all survive. No application code changed.

- **Windows** now builds four formats for x64 and arm64: the existing NSIS installer, a new **MSI**, a **portable exe** and a **zip**. The NSIS installer and the portable exe each carry **both** architectures in one file (see the naming note below); the MSI and the zips are per architecture. The MSI is built by electron-builder's WiX target, which runs only on Windows, so it is a `windows-latest` job. It installs per-user into `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\WageRoute`, so `msiexec /i … /qn` needs no elevation. WiX 4 has no arm64 architecture: the `-arm64` MSI is an x64 package carrying the arm64 app, which Windows on ARM runs normally. MSI and NSIS are **separate products** with separate uninstall entries — the release notes and README now say to uninstall one before installing the other, because no installer technology can merge them.
- **Linux** builds **AppImage**, **deb** and **rpm** for x64 and arm64. CI uses one native runner per architecture (`--linux --x64` on `ubuntu-latest`, `--linux --arm64` on `ubuntu-24.04-arm`) so `better-sqlite3` is never cross-compiled.

- **The NSIS and portable patterns carry no `${arch}` on purpose.** `NsisTarget.finishBuild` builds one installer from the requested architectures and then, only when the pattern contains `${arch}`, additionally builds one per architecture. With `${arch}` the release would have shipped three copies of each (universal plus `-x64` plus `-arm64`); without it, one file per format carrying both payloads.

On failure it lists the profile and prints the app's own `logs/main.log`. The rpm is built but not launched.

## Open gaps carried forward

1. **The import wizard's rendered flow has never been opened.** Its detection and repair rules are tested as pure code, but the wizard begins with a native file picker that CDP cannot reach. Someone has to pick a real file and walk the questions by hand — now including a real `.ofx` statement, whose rendered card and disabled **Import** button have only been reviewed, not seen.
2. **Homebrew's receipt is stale.** 1.1.0 was installed manually from the published arm64 DMG, which carries no quarantine flag, so the app opens without the Gatekeeper prompt but the cask receipt still says `1.0.2`. `brew list --versions wageroute` will disagree with the app until the next `brew upgrade --cask wageroute` — which re-installs the same 1.1.0 and, because brew downloads it, does apply quarantine.
3. **Only the arm64 macOS installer has been launched by hand.** The Intel DMG, the NSIS installer, the portable exe and the zip have never been started by a person. The MSI and the Linux AppImage/deb are booted by CI (above); an unattended runner is not the same as a desktop, so a first human launch is still worth doing before the release.
4. **The builds are unsigned.** macOS is ad-hoc signed only, so downloaders need right-click → Open; Windows shows SmartScreen and Linux package managers may warn. Signing and notarization remain undone; see [RELEASING.md](RELEASING.md#code-signing-and-notarization).

## Release checklist for the next release

Standard procedure is [RELEASING.md](RELEASING.md). These are the items that stay specific to this project:

- [ ] `package.json` → the target version on a `release/<version>` branch, via `npm version <version> --no-git-tag-version`.
- [ ] Move the `## [Unreleased]` block of [CHANGELOG.md](../CHANGELOG.md) under a `## [<version>] - <date>` heading and open an empty `Unreleased` section; update the `compare` and release links.
- [ ] Version sweep: [README.md](../README.md) header line, `curl` block and Homebrew line; [DATA-MODEL.md](DATA-MODEL.md); [FEATURES.md](FEATURES.md); [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) support table; [RELEASING.md](RELEASING.md) "current value" column; [DEPLOYMENT.md](DEPLOYMENT.md) artifact lists.
- [ ] If a migration ships: prove it against a **copy** of the live database, state in the notes that it is automatic, forward-only and irreversible, and record the new backup window.
- [ ] `npm ci` from a clean tree, `npm test`, `npm run typecheck`, `npm run build`, then **every** target: macOS DMG (arm64 + Intel), Windows NSIS (x64/arm64) and MSI (x64/arm64, task #87), Linux AppImage/deb/rpm (x64/arm64, task #38).
- [ ] Launch **each** artifact at least once on a disposable profile (`CFFIXED_USER_HOME=/tmp/...` on macOS/Linux, a throwaway Windows VM for the installers) — not on the live ledger. The Intel DMG, every Windows installer and every Linux format are still unlaunched today.
- [ ] Decide in the release notes whether MSI **ships beside** NSIS (the current config) or replaces it. Beside is the plan: they are separate products, so the notes must say to uninstall one before installing the other. CI proves an MSI reinstall stays one entry; it cannot merge an NSIS install into an MSI one.
- [ ] `SHA256SUMS.txt` for every installer, named exactly as the pinned `artifactName` produces them: `WageRoute-<version>-<arch>.dmg`, `WageRoute.Setup.<version>.exe`, `WageRoute-<version>-<arch>.msi`, `WageRoute-<version>.portable.exe`, `WageRoute-<version>-<arch>.zip`, `WageRoute-<version>-<linux arch>.AppImage`, `-<linux arch>.deb` and `-<linux arch>.rpm` (`x86_64`, `amd64` and `aarch64` are the x64 spellings), with no spaces.
- [ ] Tag `v<version>`, let CI build every platform, then publish the **draft** release CI filled with the raw installers. CI creates the draft; it never publishes.
- [ ] Bump `version` **and** every `sha256` in `ReVax-2007/homebrew-wageroute`; no `--no-quarantine`, no `zap` stanza.
- [ ] After the release: reset this document, and re-verify anything it claims.

## Compatibility notes to carry forward

| Situation | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| 1.0.2 database opened by 1.1.0 | migrates 3→4→5→6 automatically; no row opts in, every bill lands on `all_checks`, so totals and wall figures are unchanged |
| 1.1.0 database opened by 1.0.2 or earlier | not supported — no downgrade path, do not do it |
| Backup exported by 1.1.0, imported by 1.0.2 | rejected (`version 6` > the app's `SCHEMA_VERSION`) |
| Backup exported by 1.0.2 (version 3), imported by 1.1.0 | accepted; the new columns read their defaults (`NULL` = "no explicit choice", `funding_strategy` = `all_checks`) |
| Existing goal with allocation rows | keeps counting allocations until an account is bound to it; the goal screen's **Funded by** can bind it |
| Deleting a goal that owns an account | pointer nulled, the account survives and returns to the spendable pool |
| Archiving a dedicated account | progress falls back to allocation rows; the balance counts for nothing while archived |
| Emergency fund paused | settings keep the flag; a fresh install defaults to active, and a backup without the key restores as active |
| Pay cycle, payday label, default account | settings rows, not columns: a database from 1.0.2 simply lacks them and reads the defaults (`biweekly`, no label, no default account → first checking) |
| Splits stored above 100% (imported or hand-edited) | the wizard still disables **Route** with "Splits exceed 100%"; the sliders cap at what the other three leave |
